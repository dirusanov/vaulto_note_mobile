# Reddit publication media kit

Prepared 7 October 2026. These are exact copies of existing assets, with no new
image generation, bitmap edits, fabricated interactions, or changes to app screens.
This kit is prepared locally; its existence does not establish publication.

## Selection

- **Primary: `app-tour.mp4`** — H.264, 960 × 600, 19 seconds, 12 fps, no audio.
  Upload natively when the live subreddit form allows video and suitable context.
- **Alternative: `app-tour.gif`** — the same screenshot montage, about 690 KB.
  Use if GIF is supported and video is unsuitable; do not duplicate both in one post.
- **Image fallback: `01-record.png`, `02-offline.png`** — 1270 × 760 gallery cards.
  Use together where gallery uploads are supported, or the relevant single card.

Suggested disclosure in every post using the montage:

> The video is a tour of real app screenshots from v1.0.87, not a live recording.
> The current interface may differ.

## Publication boundaries

- Do not describe this as a physical-phone recording, current-build footage,
  processing-speed benchmark, or proof of task-extraction accuracy.
- Offline speech and AI require prior model download; processing speed and model
  storage requirements vary. Optional cloud processing has separate data boundaries.
- Do not promise perfectly accurate tasks: the campaign feedback includes a
  reported task-expansion issue on Pixel 7.
- Small interface text may be difficult to read in a mobile feed; explain the
  concrete purpose in the title and body.
- Confirm live community rules and allowed media before submission. Prepared
  assets and queued copy must remain distinct from published placements.
